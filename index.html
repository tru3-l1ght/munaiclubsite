About MUN AI Club

  • Aynur Sunagatullin President
  • Ivan Haievy Vice President

MUN AI Club is a student-led community for anyone interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and emerging technology. We bring students together through workshops, hands-on projects, discussions, and events that make AI more accessible, practical, and useful. Whether you’re a complete beginner or already building with AI, the club is a place to learn, collaborate, and explore what’s next.
